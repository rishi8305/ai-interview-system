Evaluation Standards

Objective, Multi-Dimensional AI Scoring Rubric

Our evaluation engine analyzes candidate responses mathematically across four distinct dimensions without demographic or visual bias.

1. Technical Correctness 40% Weight
Accuracy of concepts, facts, syntax, and logic.
2. Relevance to Prompt 25% Weight
Directness and precision in addressing what was specifically asked.
3. Completeness & Depth 20% Weight
Coverage of key sub-concepts, edge cases, and practical trade-offs.
4. Clarity & Articulation 15% Weight
Structured communication, transition words, and coherent phrasing.
